# Editorial sources and reuse

This website's English reading text is based on **Folger Digital Texts**, *Coriolanus*, Act 1, Scene 1, Folger Shakespeare Library:

https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/coriolanus/read/1/1/

Folger Digital Texts are licensed **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 3.0 Unported**:

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/

The English extract was selected, organized into bilingual speech blocks, and enhanced with interactive annotations. Some typographical presentation has changed for the digital reader. The accompanying Spanish translations and explanatory notes were independently written for this project rather than copied from a published Spanish edition.

Some textual decisions were cross-checked against the Bodleian Libraries' digitization of the 1623 First Folio:

https://firstfolio.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/

This prototype is intended for **free, noncommercial educational and literary reading**. Other modern translations, critical notes, or images should not be incorporated unless their reuse rights have been established.
